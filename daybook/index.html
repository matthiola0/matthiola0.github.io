01 / SET THE DIRECTION

Make room for a bigger goal.

Create a time-bounded goal, divide it into phases, and connect daily tasks to it. See progress and choose a completion reward that means something to you.

  • Goals with start and end dates
  • Phases and linked daily tasks
  • Progress based on completed tasks
02 / MAKE TODAY DOABLE

A routine that fits your day.

Organize tasks into day sections, repeat the work that matters, and give each habit a cue and a two-minute start. Record what happened and what you learned alongside your tasks.

  • Daily, weekly, and monthly recurrence
  • Habit cues and two-minute starts
  • Activity notes, reflections, and custom records
03 / GET HELP, KEEP THE DECISION

AI proposes. You decide.

Ask the planner to break a goal into daily work, check for overload, or adjust the next two weeks. It uses relevant calendar context to prepare a proposal. You review it before applying; applying a proposal adds content without deleting or overwriting existing items.

A few useful details

Before you begin.

Do I need an account?

Yes, to use the app. Sign in with Google or create a username and password. This product tour is public and requires no account.

What happens to my planning data?

Calendar data is scoped to your account and syncs across devices. AI requests send relevant planning context to the configured AI provider. Read the app’s privacy policy for details before using it.

Does Daybook use Claude today?

The current planner uses Groq. A proposed next step is to evaluate Claude for goal decomposition, schedule-aware proposals, and multilingual planning quality. Claude integration is not currently available, and Daybook is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

How do I share feedback?

Email hi@matthiola.dev or open an issue in the public repository. Please do not include private calendar content in public issues.
